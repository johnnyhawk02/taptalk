Make pictures with AI

In the button editor, ✨ Make picture draws a picture of the button's word using OpenAI; redo it until it's right. Get a key at platform.openai.com (it needs some credit).

Recently deleted

Backup

TapTalk keeps the board on this device only. Save everything (buttons, photos, settings) to a file now and then, for example to iCloud Drive, so nothing is lost if the iPad is lost or reset. Load the file to put the board on another iPad.

About

Most pictures are ARASAAC pictograms. They are the property of the Government of Aragón and were created by Sergio Palao for ARASAAC, which shares them under CC BY-NC-SA (non-commercial use).

Other pictures are from Mulberry Symbols © Steve Lee, used under CC BY-SA 4.0 — mulberrysymbols.org. A few extra symbols (I, you, stop, please, thank you, again) were drawn for TapTalk and are shared under the same licence.

The voice is Coral, an AI voice from OpenAI: each word is recorded once and played back, so it works offline.

Free, with no sign-up. TapTalk has no accounts and sends nothing about you or your child anywhere: the board, photos and recordings stay on this device. Searching for a picture asks ARASAAC's website; ✨ Make picture and recorded voices use OpenAI with your own key, if you add one.
